# Oreo UI changelog

Selected updates merged to main. Merge dates use Asia/Shanghai. These are not package releases or deployment announcements.

## October 15, 2026 — More pieces. More possibilities.

Brand tools, focused workspaces, and the small details that make them work.

- **Added: [Brand Guide](https://oreo-ui-preview.vercel.app/brand-guide)** — Build a brand kit with reusable colour swatches, a modular type scale, logo downloads and an email signature builder. ([PR #43](https://github.com/oreo-design/oreo-ui/pull/43))
- **Added: [Project Workspace](https://oreo-ui-preview.vercel.app/project-workspace)** — Project summaries and task rows, brought together in an editable overview, list and Kanban board. ([PR #42](https://github.com/oreo-design/oreo-ui/pull/42))
- **Added: [Tree Stepper & Security Review](https://oreo-ui-preview.vercel.app/security-review)** — A branching checklist with evidence, outcomes and a compact progress indicator that stays in sync with completed checks. ([PR #41](https://github.com/oreo-design/oreo-ui/pull/41))
- **Added: [Position Studio](https://oreo-ui-preview.vercel.app/position-studio)** — Inline asset search and a compact timeframe selector, composed into an interactive position form. ([PR #40](https://github.com/oreo-design/oreo-ui/pull/40))
- **Fixed: [Result card disclosure defaults](https://oreo-ui-preview.vercel.app/automation-components)** — AgentResultCard accepts defaultOpen for an initially expanded result while retaining controlled open and onOpenChange behavior. ([PR #29](https://github.com/oreo-design/oreo-ui/pull/29))

## October 14, 2026 — Small actions, considered.

Inline confirmation and connections that keep the next step close.

- **Added: [Inline Confirm](https://oreo-ui-preview.vercel.app/inline-confirm)** — A compact destructive-action confirmation that keeps the decision beside its trigger. ([PR #26](https://github.com/oreo-design/oreo-ui/pull/26))
- **Added: [Connections Workspace](https://oreo-ui-preview.vercel.app/connections-workspace)** — Connection rows, grouped integrations and setup surfaces composed into a complete workspace template. ([PR #24](https://github.com/oreo-design/oreo-ui/pull/24))
